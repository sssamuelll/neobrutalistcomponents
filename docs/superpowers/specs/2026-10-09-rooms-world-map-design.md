**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Owner:** Samuel Ballesteros (SDB)
**Author:** Claude, brainstormed with the owner in an interactive session with visual mockups. Every section below was presented and approved one at a time.
**Status:** Draft for owner review. Builds on `2026-10-05-neobrutalism-study-engine-design.md` (§1.2: the study is a gallery of the history of interfaces, grouped into rooms by region).

## 1. Intent (in the owner's words)

> "vamos a hacer que las salas sea un mapa mundial interactivo"

On seeing the first mockups: "el mapa tiene que ser mucho más serio, en general". Of the three serious directions: "me gusta la b y la c", and of the pairing that followed: "me gusta así, los dos".

Decisions the owner took, in order:

| Question | Owner's answer |
|---|---|
| What the map shows | **Rooms as regions.** Each room is a region of the map. No pin per work, so no coordinates are added to the theme data. |
| Map and the room cards | **Map only, with a legend.** The cards leave the Rooms page; they stay on each room's page, where they list the other rooms. |
| Style | **A dot map**, then made **serious**: real geography, Equal Earth projection, a fine grid of dots, typographic labels with coordinates, the legend as a table. |
| Light or dark | **Both.** A paper plate with sober hues in light schemes; a near-black panel with the same hues, lightened, in dark schemes. |
| Pointing and clicking | **Highlight, then go.** Pointing at a region or its legend row highlights both; a click or Enter opens the room. |
| Phones | **Map without labels; the legend leads.** Below 640 px the labels and leader lines go; the legend rows are the way in. |
| Approach | **A precomputed dot grid**, generated by a script and committed. |

What was *assumed* and stated to the owner (not corrected):
- The plate does not take the active theme's palette. Room hues on a theme's own background would vanish on themes such as Whaam! (yellow) or Amiga Workbench (blue), so the plate has fixed colours per scheme and follows only light or dark.
- Keyboard and screen-reader users go through the legend. Map regions are for pointer and touch, and hidden from assistive technology, so no link is announced twice.
- The legend keeps each room's summary as a muted line under its name.
- Origins has no place. It appears in the legend only, marked "sin lugar" / "no place".

## 2. Data

### 2.1 Source
- `world-atlas@2.0.2` as a devDependency: Natural Earth 1:110m countries as TopoJSON. Natural Earth is public domain. Used only by the generator, never shipped.

### 2.2 Generator: `scripts/build-world-map.mjs`, run by `npm run gen:map`
- Decodes the TopoJSON, projects with **Equal Earth**, drops Antarctica and the French Southern and Antarctic Lands.
- Rings that cross the antimeridian (Russia, the United States' Aleutians, Fiji) are unwrapped: the minority side moves by ±360°, then longitudes clamp to ±180°. The prototype lost Russia and Alaska without this.
- Rasterises land on a grid of pitch 5.2 over a 900 × 438 canvas, about 3,400 dots, each tagged with its room or with none.
- **Room table**, in the script: room → Natural Earth country names. `usa`: United States of America. `germany`: Germany. `italy`: Italy. `japan`: Japan. `latam`: Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean and South America, by name.
- **Anchors**, in the script: per room, a point (lon, lat) where the leader line starts (the centre of the United States, São Paulo, central Germany, Milan, Tokyo) and the label's offset in pixels, as tuned in the mockups.
- Fails loudly, naming the name, when a country in the room table is not found in Natural Earth. Nothing is dropped in silence.

### 2.3 Output: `src/site/study/world-map.generated.ts`, committed
- `viewBox`, a graticule path, one path for land without a room and one per room. Dots are small squares written as `M x y h v h z` runs, so the SVG holds a handful of paths, not thousands of nodes.
- Per room: anchor and label position in pixels, and its coordinates as text ("36°N 140°E").
- Regenerated only when rooms change, not on every build.

## 3. Page and components

### 3.1 `Scenes.tsx`
Title and lead as today, then a plate (`<figure>`) holding the map, the legend and a caption: "Proyección Equal Earth · Natural Earth 1:110m" / "Equal Earth projection · Natural Earth 1:110m", linking to Natural Earth. `SceneCards` is no longer used here; it stays in `ScenePage`.

`Scenes.tsx` holds the one piece of state: `active: Scene | null`.

### 3.2 `WorldMap.tsx`
Draws the generated data, bottom to top: graticule, land, one path per room (each inside a link to its room), labels with leader lines. Takes `active` and `onActive`; holds no state.

Pointer enter on a room sets it active, leave clears it; a click follows the link.

### 3.3 Legend
A table: Room, Works, Years.
- Room is a link, with the room's summary (`SCENE_TEXT[scene].summary`) as a muted second line.
- Works and Years are computed from `CATALOG`: the count and the first–last start year of the room's references. No new data.
- Origins last, marked "sin lugar" / "no place".
- Pointer enter or focus on a row sets its room active; leave or blur clears it.

### 3.4 Highlight
When a room is active, the other rooms' paths drop to 35% opacity and its row takes a background. The focus ring stays as the library draws it.

### 3.5 Colour
- The plate declares its own custom properties: `--map-plate`, `--map-land`, `--map-ink`, `--map-muted`, `--map-graticule` and one per room. Each uses `light-dark()`, so the plate follows the active theme's scheme and ignores its palette.
- The values live in a TS module (`PLATE`, `ROOM_HUES`) and reach the page as inline custom properties, so the contrast test reads the same values the page uses.
- Light (from the approved mockup): plate `#f2efe6`, land `#cdc7b9`, ink `#1b1b1b`, muted `#6b665c`. Rooms `usa #a8742a`, `latam #3f7a5a`, `germany #3d5a8a`, `italy #9a4a5a`, `japan #b0413e`.
- Dark: plate `#121212`, land `#3a3a3a`, ink `#ece6d6`, muted `#8a857a`. Rooms `usa #e0b066`, `latam #7cc49b`, `germany #8fb0e6`, `italy #e38fa0`, `japan #f08a84`.
- Any value that fails the contrast test in §4.3 is adjusted until it passes; the test is the rule, these values are the starting point.

### 3.6 Type
Labels and legend use the theme's monospace (`--nbc-font-mono`). Labels are uppercase with 0.08em tracking; the second line (works · years · coordinates) is smaller and muted.

### 3.7 Phones
Below 640 px: labels and leader lines are hidden, the map scales to the width, and legend rows span the full width.

### 3.8 Text
New strings in both languages: the legend's column heads, "sin lugar" / "no place", the caption.

## 4. Accessibility, errors, tests

### 4.1 Accessibility
- The SVG is `aria-hidden="true"`; region links have `tabIndex={-1}`. The legend table, with a caption, is the accessible path: every room is a link there, and focusing it highlights its region.
- The highlight never relies on colour alone: the active row changes background and keeps the focus ring; inactive regions dim.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` there are no transitions.

### 4.2 Errors
Nothing loads at run time: the map data ships in the page's chunk. There is no loading or error state to design. Every failure is caught when generating (§2.2) or in tests (§4.3).

### 4.3 Tests
- **Generated data:** every room in `THEME_SCENES` has an entry and there is none extra; every room has dots; every anchor lies inside the `viewBox`; the module is under 20 KB.
- **Contrast:** every room hue is at least 3:1 against the plate (WCAG 1.4.11, non-text), and ink and muted text at least 4.5:1, in both schemes.
- **Component:** pointing at or focusing a legend row activates its region; the legend's work counts add up to `CATALOG.length`.
- **e2e:** the Rooms test is rewritten for the map: title, one legend row per scene, a click on a row and on a region opens the room. axe and the 360 px no-sideways-scroll check already cover `/scenes` in both languages.

## 5. Out of scope
- Pins per work, and coordinates in the theme data.
- Zoom, pan or an inset.
- Another projection, or runtime map libraries.
- Rooms other than the current five; a new room needs only a line in the generator's tables and a regeneration.
